## Direction contract

THESIS: Excerly is a printed daily movement almanac — today and its practice ARE the page. Refuses the dark data-dashboard card-grid the fitness category ships.

OWN-WORLD: Night edition — warm graphite ground (#17181c), off-white ink (#ecebe4), and ONE unified brass voice (#d8a24a) for rules-ticks, stamps, today, primary action, and the active state (≤10% of any screen); a single muted brick (#d0705f) for destructive only. Display in Frank Ruhl Libre (Hebrew serif), UI/labels/numerals in Heebo; large tabular day-numerals. Editorial grid, crisp hairline rules, flat depth (no glow, no gradient), printed-stamp/tab motifs. Recognizable with all content removed: graphite + off-white + one brass, ruled grid, serif masthead. Deliberately not the AI-dark cluster (no near-black + neon + glow).

STORY: The visitor opens to today set large like an almanac masthead, reads their streak as a printed stamp and today's focus in one line, scans the month as a ruled almanac page, and taps a day to open that day's practice.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top row — "Excerly" wordmark in tracked caps (left), language toggle as a small tabbed control (right). Below, today's weekday + date set large in Frank Ruhl Libre with a one-line focus beneath; the streak as a stamped cobalt badge aligned end. A primary "start today's workout" as a bordered ink button with a cobalt underline/fill. Beneath the masthead, the month as a ruled almanac grid: weekday heads in tracked caps, big tabular day numbers, today carried by a cobalt stamp ring, activity as small ink dots.
